# Password reset and account lockout

Applies to: users who forgot their password or are locked out after too many attempts.

Steps for the user:
1. Go to the self-service password reset page (https://passwordreset.example.com).
2. Verify your identity with your registered phone or authenticator app.
3. Choose a new password: at least 14 characters, not one of your last 5 passwords.
4. Wait 5 minutes, then sign in again on your laptop and phone.

If self-service fails, the help desk will verify your identity (employee ID and manager name) and unlock the account. The help desk will never ask for your password.
