# MFA problems and new phone setup

Applies to: new phone, lost phone, authenticator app not showing codes, MFA prompts not arriving.

Steps for the user:
1. If you have your old phone, open the authenticator app and use "Transfer accounts" before wiping it.
2. If the old phone is lost, contact the help desk to clear your MFA registration after identity verification.
3. Sign in at https://mysignins.example.com and choose "Add sign-in method" to register the new phone.
4. Check that your phone's date and time are set to automatic; wrong time makes codes fail.

A lost or stolen phone that still has company email should also be reported as a security issue.
