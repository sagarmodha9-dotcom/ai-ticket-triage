# Slow laptop or computer performance

Applies to: laptop running slowly, freezing, fans loud, apps not responding.

Steps for the user:
1. Restart the laptop (not just sleep). Many issues clear after a full restart.
2. Install pending updates and restart again.
3. Close browser tabs and apps you aren't using; check Task Manager for anything using high CPU or memory.
4. Make sure at least 15% of disk space is free.

If the laptop is more than 4 years old or still slow after these steps, the help desk can schedule a hardware check or replacement.
