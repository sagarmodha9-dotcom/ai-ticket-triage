# VPN connection issues

Applies to: VPN won't connect, VPN disconnects often, can't reach internal sites while on VPN.

Steps for the user:
1. Make sure you have internet first: open a public website in your browser.
2. Fully quit the VPN client and reopen it, then sign in again with MFA.
3. Restart the laptop to apply pending updates; old VPN clients are blocked.
4. If you're on hotel or airport Wi-Fi, accept the Wi-Fi login page first, then connect the VPN.
5. If the error says "certificate expired" or "not authorized", contact the help desk.

If many users report VPN problems at the same time, treat it as an outage and escalate to the network team.
